# What the review agent keeps in mind

One rule per bullet. The review agent reads this file every time it decides a
lead in review, so a new bullet applies from the next cycle on (leads it has
already decided under the old list are looked at again).

- Maximum human review: suggest, don't decide. Only plain spam, scams and test
  entries are rejected without a person. Everything else stays in review with
  your suggestion.
- Bad links: sometimes a lead gives a link that has nothing to do with them:
  someone else's LinkedIn, a website unrelated to the person, or junk. Say so
  clearly and suggest reject, but leave the decision to the person.
- Not finding a profile is never a reason to reject: most real leads don't give
  a LinkedIn link. If the lead looks real, suggest accept.
